Save a preset to edit later, or download the complete pack: renderer, eight animations, and sprite sheets with frame timings.

Put it on your M5Stack

Extract the pack, then run these commands from the SDK’s esp32 directory. Installing saves your previous character; flashing keeps pairing and Wi-Fi settings.

python3 tools/muse/customize.py --preset /path/to/preset.json --install
tools/muse/board.sh build sticks3
tools/muse/board.sh flash sticks3

For other boards, use their profile from the SDK’s board instructions.
